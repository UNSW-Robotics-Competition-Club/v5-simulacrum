Generated by `scripts/sdk_c.py` from vexide/vex-sdk 429e0c76c4e2 (MIT) and VEX's libv5rt.a/v5_api.h. Do not edit.

These are the VEXos SDK jump-table functions (`0x037FC000`) as exposed by `sdk/` (header `include/v5_sdk.h`,
stubs `src/v5_jumptable.S`, wrappers `src/v5_sdk.c`). Nothing else in the firmware is a supported entry point:
the rest of `system_0` is internal, and MainLoop (CPU0), the FSBL, the AI Vision sensor and the smart devices
run on other processors.

552 entries: 348 with a prototype, 204 stub-only. Slot = byte offset in the
jump table. Prototypes are VEX's where it publishes one (they match the firmware where vex-sdk differs), else vex-sdk's.
